About Us

Kemix is a Saudi company headquartered in Makkah, active in general trading, general contracting, administrative and marketing services. We serve companies and institutions across Saudi Arabia, with on-site service in the Western Region cities of Makkah, Jeddah, Taif and Al-Jumum, working at the client’s site to deliver where the work is needed, and building long-term partnerships based on quality, commitment and clarity.

Our Vision

To be the first trusted partner for our clients across the Kingdom in trading, contracting and services.

Our Mission

Practical, high-quality solutions delivered on time, from the client’s site to final handover.
